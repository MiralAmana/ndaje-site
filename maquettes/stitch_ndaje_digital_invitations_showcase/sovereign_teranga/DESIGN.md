## Brand & Style
The brand personality reflects modern Senegalese celebration, prestige, and warm hospitality (*Teranga*). It positions digital stationery and interactive invitations not as basic links or PDFs, but as haute-couture digital keepsakes for milestone ceremonies: royal weddings (*Takku Sëy*), christenings (*Ngente*), galas, and high-profile corporate summits.

The aesthetic fuses **African Dark Luxury** with the precision, low-friction ergonomics of contemporary boutique design studios:
- **Tone:** Regal, intimate, warm, impeccably refined, and tech-forward.
- **Visual Influence:** Deep obsidian velvet canvases, radiant warm golds and polished bronze extracted directly from the iconic monogram, balanced by touches of sovereign emerald (*vert noble*).
- **Execution:** Minimalist composition with generous breathing room, tactile card surfaces, hair-thin geometric filigree derived from Senegalese woven patterns (*Serr*), and strict avoidance of informal elements like emojis in favor of crisp bespoke iconography.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is rooted in deep warmth and luminous metallic contrast, avoiding clinical, cold blue-grays.

### Surfaces & Backgrounds (Dark Mode Default)
- **Canvas Base:** `#0E0D0B` (Rich warm black obsidian).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Sheets):** `#171614` (Dark bronze-cast charcoal).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Modals, Overlays):** `#22201C` (Elevated warm surface).
- **Subtle Hairlines & Borders:** `#3A352D` (Subtle muted metallic contour).

### Accents & Metallics
- **Primary Accent (Champagne Gold):** `#D4AF37` — primary focal points, key headlines, active state rings.
- **Primary Gradient End (Luminous Amber):** `#E5C158` — used with `#D4AF37` for metallic gradients.
- **Secondary (Burnished Bronze):** `#8C6226` and `#5A3D14` — secondary badges, subtle glow overlays, decorative frames.
- **Action CTA Pill (Vibrant Amber Gold):** `#E5A93C` — high-contrast button fill paired with deep dark text `#0E0D0B` for instant discoverability and high accessibility.
- **Tertiary Accent (Sovereign Emerald):** `#1B4D3E` — reserved for official trust badges, RSVP confirmation states, and bilingual switch tags.

### Typography & Content
- **Text Primary:** `#FAF8F5` (Warm broken ivory, 98% opacity).
- **Text Secondary:** `#C4BCB1` (Warm champagne gray, 75% opacity).
- **Text Muted:** `#857C70` (Subtle metadata, helper text, 50% opacity).

## Typography
Typography balances statuesque modernism with effortless mobile readability across all screen densities:

- **Display & Headings (Outfit):** Delivers clean geometric shapes, open circular apertures, and sharp elegance. It ensures invitation headlines command attention without feeling archaic or rigid.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for high x-height, clear differentiation of glyphs, and superior rendering on budget-friendly Android displays prevalent across West Africa.
- **Numerals & Pricing:** All FCFA financial values must render with tabular figures (`tnum`) to keep pricing columns cleanly aligned.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column responsive grid (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) centered on a maximum container width of `1280px`.

- **Mobile First Approach:** Outer margin starts at `1.25rem` (20px) to allow maximum surface area for invitation phone mockups while preserving tap margins.
- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous vertical sections separated by `space-2xl` (64px) on mobile and `5rem` (80px) on desktop to replicate an editorial magazine experience.
- **Mockup Viewing Stages:** Single or dual interactive mobile viewport containers with fixed aspect ratio (`9:19.5`) centered within preview cards, giving clients real-time live previews of their wedding or christening invitations.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation rejects blunt drop shadows in favor of ambient warm radiance, dark layered surfaces, and micro-thin borders:

- **Surface Layering:**
  - Base: `#0E0D0B`
  - Level 1 (Card items, interactive templates): `#171614` with a `1px` border of `#3A352D`
  - Level 2 (Floating bars, drop sheets, sticky action dock): `#22201C` with `1px` border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`
- **Gold Ambient Glow:** Primary highlights and floating CTA docks use an ultra-soft golden halo: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.18)`.
- **Glassmorphism Modifiers:** Fixed headers and contextual toolbars utilize a dark backdrop blur: `background: rgba(14, 13, 11, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(16px); border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(58, 53, 45, 0.6)`.
- **Cultural Motif Dividers:** Section separators feature hairline dividers punctuated by subtle diamond or chevron nodes inspired by Senegalese weaving traditions, rendered in `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
The design system adopts a **Pill-shaped** aesthetic (`roundedness: 3`) for all action and badge elements, contrasting against sleek architectural rounded rectangles for structural viewports:

- **Buttons & Pills:** Full stadium borders (`9999px`) creating smooth touch targets.
- **Cards & Template Canvases:** `rounded-lg` (1.5rem / 24px) to mimic modern smartphone hardware bezels.
- **Phone Mockup Wrappers:** `rounded-xl` (2.5rem / 40px) matching native mobile physical enclosures.
- **Input Fields & Textareas:** `rounded-md` (0.75rem / 12px) for structured, confident form entry.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA Pill:** Background `#E5A93C`, text color `#0E0D0B` (bold weight), full radius `9999px`. Minimum height `48px`, horizontal padding `24px`. On hover, subtle luminance lift to `#F3B84E` and ambient gold shadow.
- **Secondary Metallic Outline:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#D4AF37`, text in `#FAF8F5`. On hover, surface fills with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary Pill:** Transparent background, text `#C4BCB1`. On hover, text shifts to `#FAF8F5` with background `#171614`.
- **Touch Accessibility:** All interactive tap areas strictly enforce a minimum height and width of `44px × 44px`.

### Badges & Language Switches
- **Bilingual FR / WO Indicator:** Compact pill toggle (`rounded-full`) in `#171614` with a `1px` border of `#3A352D`. Active state highlighted in deep emerald `#1B4D3E` with text `#FAF8F5`.
- **Event Category Chips:** Pills styled with `#22201C` fill and `1px` border of `#3A352D`. Text in `#C4BCB1`. Active category switches to champagne gold text `#D4AF37` and border `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`.

### Cards & Template Showcases
- **Invitation Mockup Card:** Encapsulated in surface `#171614`, surrounded by a `1px` border `#3A352D`. Houses an inner phone mockup frame displaying the animated template (music toggle, RSVP button, countdown timer, Google Maps itinerary).
- **Pricing Card (FCFA):** Features clear billing terms in Franc CFA (e.g., `45.000 FCFA`). Includes trust tags for domestic payment gateways:
  - Integrated inline payment badges: **Wave** (soft cyan badge) and **Orange Money** (warm orange-gold badge), maintaining high fidelity without cluttering the luxury dark theme.

### Form Inputs
- **Fields:** Surface `#171614`, border `1px` `#3A352D`, text `#FAF8F5`, placeholder `#857C70`.
- **Focus State:** Border transitions to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`. No default browser outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Rounded indicators (`radio: 9999px`, `checkbox: 6px`) in `#171614` with a `1.5px` border of `#5A3D14`. When selected, filled with `#D4AF37` containing a dark `#0E0D0B` checkmark or center pip.
